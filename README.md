# Student Academic Engagement & Performance Analytics Suite

## Project Overview
Educational institutions and academic leadership require data-driven visibility into student engagement, attendance patterns, and performance metrics to identify learning bottlenecks and enable early intervention for at-risk students. This project delivers a dual-page executive analytics dashboard processing 12,000+ student records to track academic outcomes, platform engagement, and submission timeliness.

![Student Academic Performance Dashboard](Student%20Academic%20Performance%20Dashboard-BI.png)

## Technical Stack & Tools
* **Business Intelligence & Visualization:** Power BI Desktop, Dynamic Matrix Layouts, Interactive Slicers
* **Spreadsheets & Databases:** Microsoft Excel (Pivot Framework), Data Quality Auditing
* **Data Engineering & ETL:** Power Query, Data Cleaning, Normalization
* **Core Competencies:** Analytical Skills, Statistics, Data Modeling, Behavioral Analytics, Stakeholder Communication

## Key Features & Business Impact
* **Large-Scale Data Engineering:** Standardized and transformed 12,000+ academic, demographic, and platform engagement records using * **Power Query**, eliminating structural anomalies and normalizing key performance metrics.
* **Dual-Page BI Architecture:** Engineered an executive overview for macro-level attendance and score tracking, paired with a granular student deep-dive page.
* **Behavioral Segmentation & Modeling:** Built relational **Data Modeling** frameworks linking platform login frequency, study hours, and assignment timeliness to overall grade outcomes.
* **Statistical Risk Identification:** Applied **Statistics** and **Analytical Skills** to establish early-warning thresholds for flagging struggling students based on attendance and assignment completion rates.

## Data Pipeline & Methodology
1. **ETL & Data Normalization:** Processed raw tabular datasets using **Microsoft Excel** and **Power Query** to resolve structural column shifts and missing values.
2. **Data Modeling & DAX:** Constructed star-schema relationships and custom DAX measures in **Power BI** to calculate rolling averages and participation scores.
3. **Executive Communication:** Formatted clear visual indicator cards and matrix views to support **Communication** with academic advisors and institution leaders
